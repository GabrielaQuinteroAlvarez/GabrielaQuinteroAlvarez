# Analog Communications – AM Modulation Lab

## Overview
This communications laboratory explored **amplitude modulation (AM)** and how modulation depth changes the shape and quality of a transmitted signal.

## Topics Covered
- Carrier signals
- Message signals
- Amplitude modulation
- Modulation index
- Under-modulation
- Correct modulation
- Over-modulation
- Time-domain waveform analysis

## Laboratory Work
During the lab, the modulation level was adjusted while observing the signal on an oscilloscope.

The waveform was compared under three main conditions:
- **Under-modulated:** message amplitude is too small
- **Correctly modulated:** envelope follows the information signal properly
- **Over-modulated:** envelope becomes distorted because the modulation index is too high

## Measurements
The lab included oscilloscope measurements and calculations used to estimate modulation depth from the AM envelope.

## What I Learned
The experiment helped connect communications equations with real waveforms. Seeing the envelope change directly made concepts such as modulation index and over-modulation much easier to understand.
